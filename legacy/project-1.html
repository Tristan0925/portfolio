Field Notes

A calmer way for distributed teams to capture, organize, and share what they learn.

Replace this image with your own project screenshot

The brief

Field Notes helps teams turn scattered observations into shared knowledge. The goal was to create an experience that feels as natural as writing in a notebook, while still giving teams the structure they need to find patterns later.

Role Design, research, development

Tools Figma, HTML, CSS, JavaScript

Year 2025

The approach

Give every idea a place to land.

Through interviews and quick prototypes, I focused the product around one simple gesture: capture now, make sense of it later. The final interface uses calm typography, clear grouping, and lightweight prompts to keep the act of documenting from feeling like administrative work.
