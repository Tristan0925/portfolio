Small Signals

Making local climate data easier to see, understand, and act on.

Replace this image with your own dashboard image

The brief

Small Signals translates environmental data into a local, human scale. Instead of overwhelming people with dashboards, it gives them a few clear signals they can use to understand their neighborhood.

Role Product design, front-end

Tools JavaScript, D3, Figma

Year 2023

The approach

Make the invisible feel close.

The experience pairs accessible data visualizations with plain-language context and small actions. I designed the interaction around comparison, so visitors can move from a broad trend to the details of the place where they live.
