Common Ground

A visual identity for a neighborhood food cooperative built around shared ownership.

Replace this image with your own project image

The brief

Common Ground needed a visual language that could feel welcoming without becoming generic. The identity makes room for many voices while staying recognizable across storefronts, packaging, and community events.

Role Art direction, identity, web

Tools Figma, Illustrator, CSS

Year 2024

The approach

Design for the table, not the spotlight.

I built the system around generous type, an earthy but lively palette, and a modular mark that could be rearranged by the community. The result is a brand that feels less like a badge and more like an invitation.
